Product Overview & Specifications

Whether you are a gigging professional, home recording artist, or serious acoustic enthusiast, the Taylor 214ce Deluxe Grand Auditorium acoustic-electric guitar delivers top-tier performance. The Grand Auditorium body shape provides versatile dynamic response suitable for both fingerpicking and aggressive flatpicking.

Equipped with Taylor’s revolutionary Expression System 2 (ES2) pickup, the guitar features three uniquely positioned and individually calibrated pickup sensors behind the saddle to capture an unprecedented range of dynamic tone.
